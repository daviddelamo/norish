Turn the recipe below into one short visual brief for an image model: a
single paragraph of at most 60 words describing what a finished serving of
this dish looks like.

- Describe only the plated dish: its colours, textures, the ingredients that
  remain visible once it is cooked, and the plate or bowl it is served in.
- Leave out amounts, ingredients that dissolve or disappear during cooking,
  and every preparation step.
- Do not name the recipe, mention cameras, lenses, styles or text, or give
  instructions. Describe the food only.

Write the brief in English, whatever language the recipe is written in.
